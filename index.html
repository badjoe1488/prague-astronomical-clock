Prazsky orloj

Old Town Hall, Prague · 1410 · still ticking

Now

Astronomical dial

Stereographic astrolabe cut for ~50N. Blue is day, brown is night. The offset ring is the ecliptic. The gilt sun rides it; the moon carries its phase. Outer schwabacher numerals are Old Czech hours counted from sunset. The crooked interior lines are temporal hours.

Calendar and zodiac

Lower disc: month labor, tropical zodiac, and the civil date. Manes painted the real one in the 1860s.

Apostle procession

On the hour from 9 to 21, or whenever you hit Walk: twelve apostles in the upper windows, Death with the hourglass, then the rooster.
